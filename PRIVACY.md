# Privacy Policy — Wheel Spin – Tap & Spin

**Effective date:** August 16, 2026

Wheel Spin – Tap & Spin ("the app") is developed by Scott Bouloutian. This policy describes how the app handles your information on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

## The short version

The app collects **no data**. None. It has no servers, no analytics, no ads, no accounts, and it never connects to the internet.

## What the app stores

The wheel options you enter and the color theme you select are saved **only on your device**, using Apple's standard local storage. This information:

- never leaves your device;
- is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party;
- is deleted automatically when you delete the app.

The iPhone app and the Apple Watch app each keep their own local copy of your options. Nothing is synced through any external service.

## What the app does not do

- No collection of personal information of any kind
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising or advertising identifiers
- No third-party SDKs or services
- No account creation or sign-in
- No network requests — the app functions entirely offline

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated at the same URL, with a new effective date, before the change ships.
